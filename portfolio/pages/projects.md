# Work

These are the software projects and research studies I have worked on. Each page explains what I built, the choices I made, and what is still unfinished.

## ResQNet

Emergency messaging between nearby phones. The Android prototype passes SOS messages directly between devices and relays them onward. Later project records describe a separate backend direction.

Prototype · Mobile and distributed systems

[Read the ResQNet case study](/projects/resqnet)

## Trading Lab

A Python workbench for testing trading rules against buy and hold, with next-open fills and a cost on every trade. A later research track writes down an opening-range protocol before testing it.

Active research · Data and evaluation

[Read the Trading Lab case study](/projects/trading-lab)

## Pancreatic CT research

A benchmark of four image backbones and nine classifiers, built around duplicate-image checks and splitting scans by patient.

Research · Medical imaging

[Read the CT research case study](/projects/pancreatic-ct)

## EEG research

A signal-processing and classification study on EEG recordings. It compares four classifiers on engineered features and keeps the research pipeline separate from any future wearable use.

Internship research · Biomedical signals

[Read the EEG case study](/projects/eeg)
