# Aaditya Adhikari

I work on software systems and applied machine learning. So far I have built an offline emergency messaging app and a trading research tool, and I have trained models on medical scans and brain signals.

[See my work](/projects) · [Read about me](/about)

## Selected work

### ResQNet

An Android prototype that passes SOS messages between nearby phones when there's no internet, along with the problems that remain.

Prototype · Mobile systems

[Read the ResQNet case study](/projects/resqnet)

### Trading Lab

A Python workbench that scores trading rules against buy and hold, with next-open fills and a cost on every trade.

Active research · Data and evaluation

[Read the Trading Lab case study](/projects/trading-lab)

### Pancreatic CT research

A medical-imaging benchmark that shows how splitting scans by patient instead of by slice changes the reported accuracy.

Research · Computer vision

[Read the CT research case study](/projects/pancreatic-ct)

## How I approach the work

Before I trust a result, I try to break the conditions it depends on. With ResQNet, I asked what an emergency app can still do when the network is gone. With Trading Lab, I test whether a trading rule still beats holding once every trade pays a cost. With my medical imaging research, I check that the model is tested on people it has never seen.

My other work includes EEG signal-processing research and an early database project in Go.
